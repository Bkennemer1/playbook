<div className="step-page-section"> 

  <h2>Overview</h2> 

  <p>The final CD is out, the file is balanced, and the borrower knows what to bring and where to be. What remains is the signing itself, the funding wire, and — critically — keeping Arive's milestones current as each happens.</p> 

</div> 

  

<div className="step-page-section"> 

  <h2>Who does what at signing</h2> 

  <ul> 

    <li><strong>The realtor coordinates the signing appointment</strong> with the borrower — date, time, location.</li> 

    <li><strong>Title sends wire instructions directly to the borrower.</strong> We don't send them (see the wire fraud warning in the previous step — make sure the borrower knows to verify by phone).</li> 

    <li><strong>The borrower brings a valid, unexpired ID</strong> and must be present to sign.</li> 

    <li><strong>Emily monitors</strong> — she's not at the signing, but she's tracking that it happened and that the file moves to funding.</li> 

  </ul> 

</div> 

  

<div className="step-page-section"> 

  <h2>Milestones through the finish</h2> 

  <p>Three milestone updates carry the file to the end, and all three are manual:</p> 

  <ul> 

    <li><strong>Docs Out</strong> — set when the final closing package goes to the borrower and title (covered in the previous step)</li> 

    <li><strong>Docs Signed</strong> — set once the borrower has signed at closing</li> 

    <li><strong>Loan Funded</strong> — set once the funding wire is confirmed sent and received</li> 

  </ul> 

</div> 

  

<div className="callout warn"> 

  <strong>Docs Signed and Loan Funded are the two most consistently missed updates in the entire process — and the consequences run further than they look.</strong> 

  <br /><br /> 

  When a milestone never gets set in Arive, nothing downstream fires. Pipeline reporting shows loans stuck at earlier stages. Funded-volume numbers are wrong. Any automation that keys off funding — including post-close borrower follow-up — simply never runs, because from every other system's perspective the loan never funded. 

  <br /><br /> 

  A loan is not finished when the wire lands. It's finished when the wire lands <em>and</em> Arive says so. Treat the milestone update as part of the funding task, not as paperwork to do later. 

</div> 

  

<div className="step-page-section"> 

  <h2>Funding</h2> 

  <p>Emily confirms the funding wire is sent and received, and a funding number is issued. That's the loan funded — and the moment the <strong>Loan Funded</strong> milestone gets set in Arive.</p> 

  <p>At that point the file exits the active pipeline and moves to <a href="/phases/post-close/thank-you-and-review">post-close</a>.</p> 

</div> 

  

<div className="step-page-section"> 

  <h2>Common pitfalls</h2> 

  

  <h3>Milestones left behind</h3> 

  <p>The big one. Docs Signed and Loan Funded get skipped in the rush to the next file. Everything downstream breaks quietly — nobody notices until the numbers don't add up at month end.</p> 

  

  <h3>Assuming the borrower verified wire instructions</h3> 

  <p>Say it explicitly, every time. The one borrower who assumed the email was legitimate is the one that costs them their down payment.</p> 

  

  <h3>Losing track after the CD goes out</h3> 

  <p>Once the final package is sent, it's easy to consider the file done and move on. It isn't done until it's funded and the milestone reflects it.</p> 

</div>
