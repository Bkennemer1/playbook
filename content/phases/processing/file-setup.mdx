<div className="step-page-section">
  <h2>Overview</h2>
  <p>File setup is the processor's opening move once the handoff lands — the back-office work that gets a disclosed file ready for underwriting. It runs alongside the borrower-facing work the same processor owns (Pre-W, doc collection, insurance intro); this step is everything else — title, appraisal, contract, CD data, and the submission package.</p>
  <p>Borrower-facing touches that come out of file setup — like the appraisal-payment heads-up below — are handled on the processor's own borrower-facing side, the same channel opened during Pre-W.</p>
</div>

<div className="step-page-section">
  <h2>Title request</h2>
  <p>The processor initiates title by <strong>emailing the title company listed on the contract</strong>, attaching the contract and the order info they need to open title.</p>
  <p><em>Scribe walkthrough pending — the title request email and what to attach.</em></p>
</div>

<div className="step-page-section">
  <h2>Appraisal</h2>
  <p>The appraisal is ordered <strong>through the lender's portal — the lender's AMC handles the assignment.</strong> The processor places the order in the portal; the AMC takes it from there.</p>

  <h3>Payment</h3>
  <p>The <strong>AMC emails the payment link directly to the borrower's email on file.</strong> The borrower shouldn't get a cold payment request from a company they don't recognize, so <strong>the processor gives the borrower a heads-up that the appraisal payment link is coming.</strong></p>
</div>

<div className="callout warn">
  <strong>Punch-list gap flagged:</strong> the "warn the borrower the AMC payment link is coming" step needs to be a standing item on the processor's task list — it isn't consistently there today. Until it's built into the punch list, make it a habit: send the heads-up whenever you place an appraisal order.
</div>

<div className="step-page-section">
  <h2>CD info with the lender</h2>
  <p>"Complete CD info" means inputting the transaction's <strong>party information into the lender portal</strong> so the lender can build the Closing Disclosure: buyer, seller, both agents, and the title company.</p>
  <p>Part of CD info is the <strong>property tax estimate/verification for escrows</strong> — getting the right tax figure so the escrow account is set up correctly. (This is what "tax analysis" refers to here — it's property-tax-for-escrow, not income tax return analysis.)</p>
</div>

<div className="step-page-section">
  <h2>Contract review</h2>
  <p>The processor reviews the executed purchase contract for:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Dates and proper execution (all signatures present)</li>
    <li>Price and terms match the 1003</li>
    <li>Option period and closing dates</li>
    <li>Seller concessions</li>
  </ul>
  <p>This is the substantive contract review — the counterpart to the Pre-W check that a contract simply <em>exists</em> in Arive.</p>
</div>

<div className="step-page-section">
  <h2>FHA / VA amendatory clause</h2>
  <p>On FHA and VA files, the processor sends the amendatory clause to the realtors. This is transaction-type-specific — conventional files skip it.</p>
</div>

<div className="step-page-section">
  <h2>VOEs (when flagged on the handoff)</h2>
  <p>When written verification of employment is flagged, the processor orders it <strong>directly to the employer</strong> — emailing or faxing the VOE form. (Not through a third-party service; direct to the employer.)</p>
</div>

<div className="step-page-section">
  <h2>Common pitfalls</h2>

  <h3>Ordering the appraisal without the payment heads-up</h3>
  <p>The borrower gets an AMC payment link from a stranger and ignores it or flags it as spam — appraisal stalls before it starts. Send the heads-up when you place the order.</p>

  <h3>CD party info entered incomplete</h3>
  <p>Missing or wrong agent/title/seller info means the lender can't build an accurate CD, which surfaces late in closing when it's most expensive to fix.</p>

  <h3>Contract terms that don't match the 1003</h3>
  <p>Price, concessions, or dates that differ between contract and 1003 are exactly what underwriting will bounce. Catch them here.</p>

  <h3>Missing the amendatory clause on FHA/VA</h3>
  <p>Easy to forget because most files are conventional. On government files it's required.</p>
</div>
