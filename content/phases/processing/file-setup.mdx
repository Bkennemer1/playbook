<div className="step-page-section">
  <h2>Appraisal</h2>
  <p>The appraisal is ordered <strong>through the lender's portal — the lender's AMC handles the assignment.</strong> Danielle places the order in the portal; the AMC takes it from there.</p>

  <h3>Payment</h3>
  <p>The <strong>AMC emails the payment link directly to the borrower's email on file</strong> — Danielle doesn't send it (consistent with the no-borrower-contact rule). But the borrower shouldn't get a cold payment request from a company they don't recognize, so <strong>Hunter gives the borrower a heads-up that the appraisal payment link is coming.</strong></p>

<div className="step-page-section">
  <h2>CD info with the lender</h2>
  <p>"Complete CD info" means inputting the transaction's <strong>party information into the lender portal</strong> so the lender can build the Closing Disclosure: buyer, seller, both agents, and the title company.</p>
  <p>Part of CD info is the <strong>property tax estimate/verification for escrows</strong> — getting the right tax figure so the escrow account is set up correctly. (This is what "tax analysis" refers to on Danielle's side — it's property-tax-for-escrow, not income tax return analysis.)</p>
</div>

<div className="step-page-section">
  <h2>Contract review</h2>
  <p>Danielle reviews the executed purchase contract for:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Dates and proper execution (all signatures present)</li>
    <li>Price and terms match the 1003</li>
    <li>Option period and closing dates</li>
    <li>Seller concessions</li>
  </ul>
  <p>This is the substantive contract review — the counterpart to Hunter's Pre-W check that a contract simply <em>exists</em> in Arive.</p>
</div>

<div className="step-page-section">
  <h2>FHA / VA amendatory clause</h2>
  <p>On FHA and VA files, Danielle sends the amendatory clause to the realtors. This is transaction-type-specific — conventional files skip it.</p>
</div>

<div className="step-page-section">
  <h2>VOEs (when flagged on the handoff)</h2>
  <p>When written verification of employment is flagged, Danielle orders it <strong>directly to the employer</strong> — emailing or faxing the VOE form. (Not through a third-party service; direct to the employer.)</p>
</div>

<div className="step-page-section">
  <h2>Common pitfalls</h2>

  <h3>Ordering the appraisal without a Hunter heads-up</h3>
  <p>The borrower gets an AMC payment link from a stranger and ignores it or flags it as spam — appraisal stalls before it starts. Confirm Hunter has warned them.</p>

  <h3>CD party info entered incomplete</h3>
  <p>Missing or wrong agent/title/seller info means the lender can't build an accurate CD, which surfaces late in closing when it's most expensive to fix.</p>

  <h3>Contract terms that don't match the 1003</h3>
  <p>Price, concessions, or dates that differ between contract and 1003 are exactly what underwriting will bounce. Catch them here.</p>

  <h3>Missing the amendatory clause on FHA/VA</h3>
  <p>Easy to forget because most files are conventional. On government files it's required.</p>
</div>
